# One Word From Grandma — candidates

Competition: u-0xkianno-one-word-from-grandma-f04bed16 ("One Word From Grandma", make.fun)
Brief: the single word your grandma would text about AI. Judge model compares two
words at a time and picks the funnier, more grandma-like one. Criteria in order:
(1) reads as the word a grandma would text about AI, (2) real grandma texting voice,
warm and a little baffled, (3) funny in one word with no context, (4) tied to AI /
robots / machines, (5) fresh rather than obvious, still instantly readable.

## Candidates (drafted fresh in this session)

1. `robit` — the typo grandma would actually make; cute, baffled, directly about robots.
2. `witchcraft` — grandma's verdict on AI: it must be magic. Funny with zero context.
3. `sorcery` — same magic angle as witchcraft, slightly softer and less punchy.
4. `beepboop` — grandma imitating robot noises; warm and silly, but more robot than grandma.
5. `doohickey` — peak grandma word for a thing she doesn't understand; weak AI tie.
6. `thingamajig` — same family as doohickey; grandma-voice strong, AI tie weak.
7. `whatchamacallit` — classic baffled-grandma word; too long, no AI tie.
8. `contraption` — grandma's word for a machine; decent AI tie, less funny.
9. `newfangled` — grandma complaining about new tech; funny, but fits any new thing.
10. `humbug` — dismissive grandma energy; funny, but not AI-specific at all.
11. `malarkey` — old-timey grandma dismissal; same problem as humbug.
12. `gizmo` — grandma-ish gadget word; short and readable, but a bit flat.
13. `whatsit` — baffled grandma shorthand; funny, weak AI tie.
14. `knowitall` — grandma calling the AI a know-it-all; human, funny, AI-tied.
15. `hocuspocus` — magic angle again; playful, but reads more like a kid's word.
16. `skynet` — grandma watched Terminator once; too nerdy, not grandma-voice.
17. `terminator` — the obvious movie reference; fails criterion 5 (obvious).
18. `robot` — the obvious word; fails criterion 5 and has no joke.
19. `gadget` — plain and safe; no joke, no grandma voice.
20. `fiddlefaddle` — warm old-fashioned nonsense word; funny, but fits any subject.

## First pick

`robit` — strongest on criteria 1, 2 and 4: it is literally the typo a grandma
would make while texting about a robot, warm, baffled, and directly tied to AI.
Written to main.py first, verified locally, then revised (see below).

## Revision

On reflection, `robit` has a real weakness: it is the most predictable typo-pick in
the field, so many entrants will submit the same bytes — byte-identical entries
share one rate and the earlier receipt places above its copies. `witchcraft`
keeps the grandma voice (her verdict on AI: it must be magic), lands a bigger
joke with no context (criterion 3), is still tied to AI-as-magic (criterion 4),
and is far fresher and less likely to be duplicated (criterion 5). A grandma
texting "witchcraft" about AI is exactly the warm, baffled voice the judge is
briefed to reward.

## Final pick

`witchcraft`
